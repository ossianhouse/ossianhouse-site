What we're building

Nomentra

A private travel record for people who cross borders often.

It builds an accurate day-by-day history of where you have been from evidence already on your phone, and keeps that history with you.

From that history it follows the limits that matter — visa, residency and tax day counts, including rules you set yourself — and shows where you stand, and how planned travel would change it.

Facts and estimates are never mixed together. In development for iOS.
