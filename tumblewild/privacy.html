Tumblewild privacy

Your play stays on your device. Beta feedback is your choice.

Last updated 31 October 2026 · Current Tumblewild beta

What the app stores

Tumblewild stores worlds, personal toy colours and settings locally on your iPhone or iPad so you can continue playing. The current beta does not upload these creations to Ossian House Holding Ltd and has no cross-device sync.

Depending on your device settings, Apple may include local app data in device backups. Those backups are managed through Apple, not a Tumblewild account.

No accounts, advertising or tracking

The current beta has no sign-in, advertising, in-app purchases, chat, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not ask children for names, email addresses, photos, location or other personal details in the app.

Tilt play

When you use Tilt, the app reads the device's motion to move toys with the direction of gravity. That motion is processed on the device and is not sent to us.

Apple TestFlight

TestFlight is an Apple service, separate from the app's local play. Apple automatically collects and shares crash logs and usage information with us during TestFlight testing, including sessions and device and operating-system details. You can also choose to send feedback. Depending on how you join and submit feedback, this can include your name, email address, comments and screenshots.

See Apple's TestFlight information and TestFlight and Privacy for details. Parents or guardians should manage beta participation; no child needs to send us feedback or identify themselves.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. Our mailbox is provided by Google. We use support and beta feedback to answer you and improve the app. Please leave children's personal details out of messages and screenshots.

Email remains in our mailbox until deleted. You can ask us to delete your messages or tester information we hold by contacting developer@ossianhouse.com. Apple's own records and device backups are governed by Apple's policies.

Your choices

You can stop testing through TestFlight. Deleting Tumblewild from the device removes its local app data; any backups you keep are managed separately through Apple. You do not need to send feedback to play.

This website and future changes

This page is hosted on the company website. Its hosting and contact handling are described in the website privacy notice. This policy describes the current beta; if data handling changes, we will update this page and its date.
