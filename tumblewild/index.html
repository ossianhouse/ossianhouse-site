A toy box for curious play

Put a Cat, Rubber ball, Block, Spring, Fan and Bubble together and experiment. Build a perch, try a bounce, or see where the wind takes a Bubble.

The current preview includes free play, three editable starting games, and My Toys, where you can choose colours for your own Cat and use it in different worlds.

Designed for children aged 4–8, with optional grown-up company. Tumblewild is still in development; movement, interactions and ease of use are being tested.

Family testing

External testing is being prepared through Apple TestFlight. Invitations will be shared separately when a suitable build has been approved. Tumblewild is not yet available on the App Store.

The current app requires iOS 26 or later on iPhone, or iPadOS 26 or later on iPad. A parent or guardian should handle the invitation and TestFlight setup on an eligible account and device.

There is no Tumblewild account, advertising, in-app purchase or chat in this preview. Worlds and personal toy colours are saved on the device; cross-device sync is not included.

Support and feedback

Email developer@ossianhouse.com. A parent or guardian can tell us which device and app version they used, what they tried, and what happened.

For play feedback, the most useful things are what the child chose to try, what confused them, and what they wanted to do again. No child's name, photo, voice recording or other personal details are needed.

You can also use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight. Please check screenshots before sharing them and leave out personal information.
